The Fjord Detectives

Privacy policy

Updated 9 October 2026

The Fjord Detectives does not collect any personal data.

What the game stores

The game stores only on your own device: your chosen language, sound settings, which cases you have solved and how far you have got. This never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else.

No tracking, no ads

The game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The “Whole first season” and “Whole second season” purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales figures with us.

Children

Because we collect nothing, we collect nothing about children either.

Contact

Privacy questions: norgik@hotmail.com. Developer: Konstantin Krane, Norway.

Changes

If the game ever changes how it uses data, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
