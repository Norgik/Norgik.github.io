The Fjord Detectives

Privacy policy

Updated 26 October 2026

The Fjord Detectives does not collect any personal data. To make the game better, it sends anonymous statistics about how far players get.

What the game stores

The game stores only on your own device: your chosen language, sound settings, which cases you have solved and how far you have got. This never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Anonymous game statistics

To see where players get stuck, the game sends us short anonymous events: the game was opened, a case was started, a place in a case was finished, the accusation board was reached, the case was solved, the hints ran out, the purchase screen was shown, a purchase was made or cancelled.

Each event carries only the game, its version, the game language, the App Store country and a random number created when the game is installed. It never contains your name, email, Apple ID, advertising identifier or location, and we do not use your IP address to work out where you are. The events are stored by PostHog on servers in the EU and are used only to improve the game. If you delete the game, the random number is deleted too; a new installation gets a new one.

No tracking, no ads

The game has no ads and no accounts. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Purchases

The “Whole first season” and “Whole second season” purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales figures with us.

Children

We collect no personal data from anyone, children included. The anonymous statistics cannot identify a child.

Changes

If the game ever changes how it uses data, we will update this page before the change takes effect. 26 October 2026: anonymous game statistics added.
